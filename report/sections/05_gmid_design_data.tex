\subsection{Transconductance Efficiency}

The quantity

\begin{equation}
	\frac{g_m}{I_D}
\end{equation}

provides a direct relationship between transistor bias current and
transconductance.

It is therefore used as one of the principal parameters for selecting
the MOS transistor inversion level.
