\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This project establishes a compact characterization reference for the
SKY130A MOS devices most relevant to the planned analog design work.

The standard \qty{1.8}{\volt} NMOS and PMOS were characterized in terms
of drain current, threshold and saturation voltages,
transconductance efficiency, and gate capacitance.

The \code{nfet\_03v3\_nvt} and
\code{nfet\_05v0\_nvt} were additionally characterized to provide
low-threshold alternatives for circuits requiring native-device
operation.

The report intentionally concentrates on practical design quantities
rather than providing an exhaustive characterization of the complete
PDK. Device families such as the high-voltage MOS devices can be added
when a future circuit---for example a CIS architecture---actually
requires them.

The quick-reference table in
Section~\ref{sec:device_quick_reference} is intended to provide the
main entry point for future device selection, while the detailed plots
remain available when bias- or geometry-dependent information is
required.
